# Data

This folder documents the datasets used in the project. It may also hold small, non-sensitive files (e.g. a study-area boundary or a short site list).

## What is not stored here

Large and raw Earth observation datasets are **not** kept in this repository. Landsat and Sentinel imagery will mostly be accessed directly through Google Earth Engine. Any large local files are stored outside GitHub.

The `.gitignore` excludes `data/raw/`, `data/interim/`, and common large raster and archive formats (e.g. `.tif`, `.nc`, `.zip`).
